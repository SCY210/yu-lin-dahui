# Community site explanation and privacy notice

The owner states that the website is maintained voluntarily by Yu Lin Da Hui club members and does not pursue profit. Shared public wording lives in `lib/site-notice.ts`, appears in the footer and `/terms`, and is included in the canonical activity notice. This describes the website, not the legal identity of a controller or the commercial status of venues and other third parties.

The explanation covers service interruptions, checking arrangements and calculations, voluntary extended profiles, authenticated-member visibility, and a practical request route through the existing club contact channel. The personal profile links to `/privacy`. Essential signup and result records are distinguished from optional profile fields. No new tracking, consent flags, permission changes or automatic erasure mechanism are introduced.

Privacy language preserves applicable rights and leaves room for lawful record retention and other participants' rights. It does not promise immediate deletion of every historical fact, unconditional immunity, verified identity, notarized evidence or complete regulatory compliance. Account-level agreement must not be treated as blanket consent for unrelated processing, marketing or public photo distribution.

Activity notice version `2026-10-12-v4` describes one-time account entry confirmation and checkbox-free signup. Accounts without any valid historical confirmation are asked on entry; a previously confirmed account can enter without another prompt. Updated text remains readable without relabeling old receipts as current agreement. Signup creates no additional consent records. The separate photo-rights version is unchanged because its confirmation text and scope are unchanged.

The actual operator identity, applicable processing bases, retention criteria, processor/transfer details and other incomplete configuration remain explicitly incomplete. A volunteer/nonprofit statement is not a substitute for those facts. Do not invent them or remove the incompleteness indicator without verified configuration.

Wording references: [AEPD layered information guidance](https://www.aepd.es/derechos-y-deberes/conoce-tus-derechos/derecho-de-informacion) and [AEPD erasure guidance](https://www.aepd.es/derechos-y-deberes/conoce-tus-derechos/derecho-de-supresion-al-olvido). These sources inform the wording and do not certify this deployment.

The entry confirmation of the activity notice has since been retired; see [the account notice gate](account-notice-gate.md).
