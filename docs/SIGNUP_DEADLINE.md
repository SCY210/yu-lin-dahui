# Sign-up availability

There is no separate member sign-up deadline or deadline field. Members may join an open activity while their chosen court slot is still running. New activities keep `signupDeadline` equal to their end time for compatibility; no two-hour cutoff is imposed on current or historical activities.

Home offers a reminder for an upcoming activity within 24 hours, and the Activities tab groups open, unjoined activities without an early cutoff. Cancellation retains its separate one-day rule. The original court signup regression tests exercise late signups.
